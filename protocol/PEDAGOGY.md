# Pedagogy Contract 0.1

## Rules for package compilation

1. **Retrieval practice:** require active recall before restudy when prior exposure exists.
2. **Spacing:** preserve due constraints and obtain later evidence before stability claims.
3. **Generation before explanation:** ask the learner to answer, write, speak, select, or justify before full explanation.
4. **Correction loop:** attempt, feedback, learner correction, then retry; the retry remains separately evidenced.
5. **Task repetition:** speaking and writing improvement requires another production attempt, not passive review.
6. **Transfer:** training success is not mastery; test a changed or unfamiliar context.
7. **Interleaving:** mix skills only when the contrast serves discrimination or retrieval, never by mechanical randomness.
8. **Difficulty calibration:** choose a meaningful challenge with a reasonable success probability and no deliberate reward farming.
9. **Deliberate practice:** prioritize a high-leverage bottleneck or uncertainty-reducing test over equal allocation.
10. **Assessment integrity:** assessment runs without hints, correction, retry, adaptive easing, or reward reveal during performance.
11. **Subjective feedback checkpoint:** content-dependent Writing and Speaking feedback is produced by the External Coach only after the learner artifact is durably evidenced; Runtime enters `AWAITING_COACH` and waits for a validated continuation.

## Evidence interpretation

Self-rating is metacognitive evidence only. Training, transfer, and assessment observations are stored separately. A single success cannot establish mastery or a breakthrough. Writing and speaking estimates include rubric dimensions, confidence, evidence references, producer, and producer version; they never claim official examiner status.

## Version recording

Every package records the complete contract set defined by `VERSIONING.md`, enabling later replay and an explicit unsupported-replay result.
